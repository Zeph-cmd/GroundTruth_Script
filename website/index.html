What GroundTruth gives you

Cryptographic Integrity

Every capture is sealed with a SHA-256 manifest. If anything is altered, the evidence score drops.

Authenticity Score

0–100 score based on GPS gaps, timestamp regularity, and integrity failures.

GPS Route Mapping

Visualize every trip on an interactive map with start/end markers and stale-GPS detection.

AI Detection

YOLOv8-powered detection of road defects and objects in your field imagery.

Dual App

GroundTruth Vision (Android) collects evidence; GroundTruth Explorer (Windows) analyzes it.

Kill Switch

Remote deactivation for misuse — your control stays with you.
